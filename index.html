Inputs

Shamir secret sharing

The Shamir of the device (OPSEC menu): each share is a BIP39 mnemonic plus its share ID, and any k shares give the seed back. Write the ID next to each share: without it the share cannot be used.

Operation

The random coefficients come from this browser's random numbers. The device takes them from the timing of the keys pressed instead.

Cards

MNEMONIC TO CARDS of the device: the seed kept as the order of a few decks of cards. The order is Seedmate's own format: this page (or the device) turns it back into the words.

Direction

Rank A 2-9 T J Q K (10 also works) and suit C D H S, separated by spaces, as the device shows them.

Build your own recovery tool

You do not have to trust this page, or the device. Everything Seedmate writes is described below, exactly, with test vectors computed by the firmware's own code. Give them to an AI assistant (or to a programmer) and get your own recovery tool, which you can read and check.

  1. Copy the prompt, the specification and the test vectors with the button below (or take the files of the folder build-your-own of this page's repository). In the prompt, write which functions you need.
  2. Paste it into your AI assistant and let it write one HTML file. Never paste real words or shares into an AI chat: it does not need them, and the vectors are enough to test.
  3. Open the file on a computer without network and press its Self test: every vector must pass. If one fails, give the failure back to the assistant until all pass.
  4. Try it with a throwaway seed: make shares, an obfuscated seed or a card order of it on your device, and recover them with your tool.
  5. Only then use it with your real backups, and only offline.
